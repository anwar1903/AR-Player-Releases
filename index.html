Direct3D 11 GPU Acceleration

Powered by libmpv (d3d11va) with a 32 MiB demuxer read-ahead buffer for stutter-free 1080p & 4K playback on integrated graphics.

🔒

100% Offline & Private

Works seamlessly in Airplane Mode. No cloud dependencies, no analytics, and no background network calls during media playback.

🎵

Music Mode & Mini-Player

Automatically extracts embedded album art and metadata for .mp3 and .flac tracks, plus an always-on-top compact mode (W).

📂

Folder Queues & Auto-Resume

Drag and drop entire movie or music folders. Playback timestamps save locally every 5 seconds so you never lose your spot in long films.

💬

Fuzzy Subtitles & Sync

Auto-detects external .srt / .ass files in Subs\ folders, switches tracks on the fly, and fine-tunes delay in 0.1s steps (G / H).
